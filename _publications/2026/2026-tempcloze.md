abstract: >-
  We introduce TempCloze, a video cloze benchmark for evaluating visual temporal reasoning in Video-LLMs by asking them to identify the true missing middle between beginning and ending clips. Across 1,521 videos and 31 Video-LLMs, TempCloze reveals temporal alignment as the primary bottleneck.

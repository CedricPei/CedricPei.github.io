abstract: >-
  We present ThunderWorld, a training-free framework that selectively corrects W4A4 quantization errors in world models using dense native FP4 matrix products. On Cosmos3 Edge and Nano, it preserves generation quality close to BF16 while achieving 1.17–1.88× the throughput of ARCQuant (2.0) on RTX PRO 6000 and Jetson AGX Thor.

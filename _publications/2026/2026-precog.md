abstract: >-
  We introduce PreCoG, a framework that diagnoses semantic failures in low-cost video previews and corrects cached generation trajectories before final rendering. By combining PreQA assessment with PreEdit correction, it reduces redundant computation while preserving already correct content, achieving strong video quality and alignment at lower inference cost.

abstract: >-
  We introduce ROSE, an intent-centered evaluation metric for NL2SQL that uses an adversarial Prover-Refuter cascade to judge whether predicted SQL captures a user's intent. ROSE aligns more closely with expert assessments and supports a large-scale re-evaluation of 19 NL2SQL methods.

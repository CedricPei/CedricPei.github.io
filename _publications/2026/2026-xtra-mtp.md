abstract: >-
  We present Xtra-MTP, a native multi-token prediction framework for hybrid language models that combines one-token alternatives, compact recoverable states, and parallel Gated DeltaNet verification. Across three Qwen models and six workloads, it improves throughput by 29.4–39.2% over the best tested SGLang configurations under a 30 ms tail-latency constraint.

abstract: >-
  We introduce HALO, a human-grounded framework for evaluating whether, when, and how far proactive agents should assist. HALO-Arena spans four proactive need types across ten everyday domains, while HALO-8K contains 8,000 responses from ten models annotated by twenty experts. Granular human judgments of timing, intensity, and acceptability reveal over-proactivity as the dominant observed failure mode.
